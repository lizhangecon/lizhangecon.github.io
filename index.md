## Welcome to my page!

Hi, I am Li Zhang (张力), an Assistant Professor in the [Faculty of Business Administration](https://fba.um.edu.mo) at the [University of Macau](https://www.um.edu.mo). Before joining UM, I worked as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the [ROCKWOOL Foundation Berlin](https://www.rfberlin.com) (RFBerlin) and the [Centre for Research and Analysis of Migration](https://www.cream-migration.org) (CReAM) at [University College London](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/social-historical-sciences/economics) (UCL). I received my Ph.D. in Economics from [The Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://www.econ.cuhk.edu.hk) (CUHK) in 2024 and obtained my Bachelor's and Master's Degrees in Economics from [Renmin University of China](https://ae.ruc.edu.cn) (RUC).

My research interests include labor economics, innovation, development economics, and economics of education.
